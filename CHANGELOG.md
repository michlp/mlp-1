# Changelog

## Issue 2 — draft
Adds Rules 2.9, 5.7, 6.7, 6.8, 6.9 and 9.9, and GR-11. Adds an order of
precedence and a note on conformance and construction to the General
introduction. Revises Rules 2.8, 3.1, 3.2, 3.6, 5.1, 6.5, 6.6, 7.3 and GR-4.
Closes the `should` carve-out in Rule 3.1. `Should` was permitted in R3 and
R4 explanatory text where the source was hortatory, a term the standard never
defined. It is now unavailable at every register. A hortatory source is
reported by the attributed form under Rule 10.7, or quoted under Rule 2.7.
Adds a declaratory mapping for `shall` to Rules 3.2 and 10.7: a shall that
states an amount or what is treated as what takes the simple present and
carries no operator. Adds a note to Rule 10.7 that an inverted conditional
`should` ("Should the court desire") means `if` and is not an operator at
all. Both surfaced from restating real court rules.
Corrects the scope of the Plain Writing Act in the reference documents.
Removes the Independence and attribution section from the Notices.
79 rules, 11 general recommendations.

Derived from research into 40 state legislative drafting manuals across 37
states, federal administrative drafting authority, and the plain-language
jury instruction projects. See `research/`.
