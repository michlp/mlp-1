# Requesting designation to the Davidson County Business Court Docket

Register R2. Sources: the 2015 pilot order, the 2017 Phase 2 order, and SCT
Rule 11(I), effective 2019-11-01.

## Requesting designation

The 2015 order and the 2017 order both allow any party to file a Request for
Designation. They set different deadlines and different filing offices, and
this summary does not resolve which governs.

Under the 2015 order, any party may file the request within 60 days after a
complaint is served on a defendant. That order directs the request to the
Business Court.

Under the 2017 order, any party may file the request within 30 days after a
complaint is served on a defendant. That order directs the request to the
Davidson County Chancery Court, and requires a copy served on all parties.

The two orders conflict on the period and on the filing office. Whether the
2017 order displaces the 2015 order, and for which cases, is a question of
construction that this summary does not answer.

## Who decides, and on what

Under the 2015 order, the Chief Justice determines whether a case meets the
eligibility criteria, upon the recommendation of the Business Court Judge. If
the case meets the criteria, the Chief Justice may transfer it.

Under the 2017 order, the Chief Justice determines whether a case meets the
eligibility criteria. The Chief Justice also determines whether the case is
sufficiently complex to warrant transfer, in the discretion of the Chief
Justice. The Chief Justice may then transfer the case.

The 2017 order does not state a recommendation step. Neither order states
whether the recommendation of the Business Court Judge survives.

## Objecting to assignment

Under the 2015 order, an objection to assignment must be filed with the Chief
Justice within 30 days after entry of the order transferring the case. That applies to every objection except one going to eligibility. The 2015
order does not state who must file the objection.

## Assignment of transferred cases

Under SCT Rule 11(I), cases transferred to the Business Court Docket are
assigned to Chancellor Anne C. Martin of the Davidson County Chancery Court
Part II. That applies from 2019-11-01.

A case transferred before 2019-11-01 remains as previously assigned.

The Supreme Court may designate other judges, including senior judges, to
preside over particular cases or judicial settlement conferences. It may do so
in its discretion and as necessary.

Outside Davidson County, the Court may appoint a Senior Judge to hear an
eligible case in the venue where it was originally filed. The Court may do so
at the request of a trial court judge. The parties may instead request transfer
of venue to the Davidson County Business Court Docket.
