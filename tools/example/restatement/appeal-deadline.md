# Notice of appeal — filing and record transmittal

Fed. R. App. P. 3 and Rule 4(a)(1)(A) govern the notice of appeal in a civil
case.

The notice of appeal must be filed with the district clerk within 30 days
after the judgment or order is entered. The rule does not state who must file
the notice. The rule states no consequence for a late filing.

Standing Order Section 2 states that counsel "shall" transmit the record to
the clerk of the court of appeals. Courts have divided on whether that word
imposes a duty or states a preference.
